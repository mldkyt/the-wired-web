# Pleroma

The Pleroma instance is available from [the-wired.club](https://the-wired.club) (the root domain).

## Rules

We have really basic rules just to get the worst of the worst out

1. common sense
2. use content warnings for topics like politics, food and lewd content, or anything that would require a content warning
3. mark media that's sensitive as sensitive (implicit with cw's but sometimes you might wanna do it)
4. no nazis(m), fascis(m|t) or marxist-leninis(t|m) content/people allowed here

## How do I use CW's

Basically you should set a CW on the following content:

- Anything that could make anyone for example uneasy, like eye contact, lewd content, food, politics or anything you can imagine, you should set a CW to that.
- Any post that is longer than normal (the instance has 100000 post limit), set a CW containing the subject of the post and put "long" at the end

## Reporting

Reporting the content will forward a report to me, I will take a look at it and take action. Don't hesitate to report stuff, nothing here is powered with AI.
